Your data stays on your phone

Sales, cash, credits, stock, staff, invoices, photos of papers and settings are stored only on the phone. There is no account with us, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Optional cloud backup

If the business turns on cloud backup, the phone encrypts all data with a password chosen by the business (AES-256) before sending it to the backup server shown in the app. The server only stores files it cannot read; nobody can open them without that password, including us. The last 30 copies are kept. To delete them, contact us at the address above.

When the app uses the internet

  • Cloud backup, if turned on (encrypted, see above).
  • IFB payments, if the business turns them on: when a customer pays on the business’s Infinite Future Bank page, IFB sends the backup server the amount, the fee, the reference typed by the customer and the payer’s name. The server keeps these for 90 days so the phone can match the payment; it does not keep the payer’s e-mail or other answers. Card and mobile-money details are handled by IFB, never by this app.
  • “Read the numbers” on a photo: the reading engine is downloaded once from a public content network (jsDelivr). The photo itself is read on the phone and is not sent.
  • WhatsApp or SMS reminders and receipts: the app opens WhatsApp or SMS with a message that you choose to send.

Phone permissions

  • Camera: only to photograph receipts and papers, when you tap the button.
  • Bluetooth: only to print receipts on a printer you choose.

Deleting data

Settings → Training → Erase everything, or uninstall the app. Cloud backups: contact us, or the business can stop backups in Settings → Cloud backup → Disconnect.

The app is a business tool and is not meant for children.
